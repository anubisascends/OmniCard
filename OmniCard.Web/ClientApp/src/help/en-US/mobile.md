# Using OmniCard on a phone

OmniCard works in your phone's browser, with layouts made for small screens. You can look up cards, check where they are and what they're worth, and scan new cards with your phone's camera. You can also add OmniCard to your home screen so it opens like an app.

## Finding your way around

A bar along the bottom of the screen takes you to the first four of **Dashboard**, **Scan**, **Intake**, **Collection** and **Locations** that you're allowed to see. Tap **More** for every other section and for **Help**.

The game selector and the account menu stay in the top bar.

![OmniCard on a phone, with the bottom bar and the More menu open](getting-started-phone-menu.png)

## Add OmniCard to your home screen

Adding OmniCard to your home screen gives it its own icon and opens it full screen, without the browser's address bar.

**Android (Chrome)**

1. Open OmniCard in Chrome and sign in.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**, then confirm.

**iPhone or iPad (Safari)**

1. Open OmniCard in Safari and sign in.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

> [!NOTE]
> Phones only install OmniCard as a full-screen app when it's opened over a secure (https) address. Over a plain http:// address you may only get a shortcut that opens in the browser. Ask your administrator if you're not sure which address to use.

## Looking up cards

On the [Collection](/collection) page and on a location's page, cards are shown as a list instead of a table. Each card shows its picture, name, set and number, condition, language and foil badges, location, market price and quantity.

- **Search** with the box at the top, just as on a computer. See [Search syntax](help:search-syntax).
- Tap **Sort** to choose what the list is sorted by. Pick the same choice again to reverse the order.
- **Stack** and **Group by** work as they do on a computer. See [Collection](help:collection).
- Tap a card to open its details, where you can change its condition, quantity, location and more.
- **Press and hold** a card to see a large picture of it. Tap anywhere to close the picture.

![The collection list on a phone](mobile-collection.png)

To work with several cards at once, tap **Select**, tap the cards you want, then tap **Actions** for **Move to…**, **Bulk edit**, **List for sale**, **Export CSV**, **Print labels** and **Delete**.

On a location's page, **Add card** stays at the top. Tap **⋮** beside it for **Audit**, **Print labels** and **Import**. On the [Locations](/locations) page, tap **New location** to add one.

## Scanning with your phone

On the [Scan](/scan) page, tap **Take photo** to photograph a card with your phone's rear camera. Each photo is matched and added to the review list.

Tap a card in the list to open it full screen. **Confirm match** confirms it and moves on to the next card that needs review. See [Reviewing on a phone](help:scanning#reviewing-on-a-phone).

## Good to know

- Wide dialogs open full screen on a phone. Close them with their **Cancel**, **Close** or **Done** button.
- On the **Sales** page, swipe the order board sideways to move between columns.
- In **Administration**, pick a page from the **Section** list at the top.
- Dragging cards between binder pockets needs a mouse. Use a computer to rearrange binders.
