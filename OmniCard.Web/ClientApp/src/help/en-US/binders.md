# Binders

The binder view shows a binder location the way it looks on your shelf: two facing pages of pockets, with each card in its own page and slot. Use it to flip through a binder, see what's for sale, and arrange cards into pockets.

## Open a binder

1. Go to [Locations](/locations).
2. In the **Binders** group, click the binder's name. Binders open straight into the binder view.

You can also open a binder's regular location page and click **Open binder view**. To get back to the card table, click the binder's name in the page path at the top (**Locations ▸ *binder name* ▸ Binder**).

Need a new binder? Create a location with the type **Binder**. See [Locations](help:locations#create-a-location).

![A binder open to a two-page spread](binders-spread-view.webp)

## Flip through the binder

The binder is shown one spread (two facing pages) at a time, just like opening a real binder:

- The first spread shows page 1 on its own, on the right.
- After that, each spread shows an even page on the left and the next odd page on the right (pages 2–3, 4–5, and so on).

To move around:

- Click **‹ Prev** or **Next ›** to turn one spread at a time.
- Click a tab in the page strip (labelled *1*, *2–3*, *4–5*…) to jump straight to that spread. On narrow screens the strip scrolls; use its arrows to see more tabs. On a phone, the two pages of a spread are shown one above the other.

The heading shows which pages you're looking at and how many pages the binder has, for example *Pages 2-3 · 12 pages*. Once the binder has dividers, it also shows the section you're in, for example *Pages 4-5 · 12 pages · Showcase*.

## Dividers

Dividers work like the tabbed dividers in a real binder: each one has a name and marks where a section starts, so you can jump straight to it. For example, split a Magic binder into *Borderless*, *Showcase* and *Extended art*, then by color inside each.

A divider is only a bookmark in the binder. It never moves or changes a card.

![A binder with two dividers: the Showcase tab is highlighted and its divider sits between the facing pages](binders-dividers.webp)

### Add a divider

1. Click **Edit**, then turn to where the section starts. If you need room, add a page first (see [Pages and layout](#pages-and-layout)).
2. Click **Add divider** in the edit toolbar.
3. Type a name, for example *Borderless · Blue*, and choose a **Tab color** if you like.
4. Click **Save**, or press Enter.

Where the divider goes:

- **In the two-page view**, it goes between the two facing pages, in front of the right-hand page. On the first spread (page 1 on its own) or a last spread with only a left page, it goes in front of that one page.
- **In Simplified editing**, it goes in front of the page you're on.

### Jump to a divider

The dividers are shown as a row of tabs above the pages, in binder order, each with the page its section starts on (for example *p. 12*). They stay there whichever page you're on, in edit mode and out of it.

- Click a tab to turn to it. In the two-page view you see the spread the divider sits in; in Simplified editing you see the divider's page itself.
- A tab is raised and filled with its color while its divider is on screen: on either page of the spread, or on the page you're on in Simplified editing. Turn past it and the tab goes back to normal. The name of the section you're in still shows next to the page number at the top.
- On the page, a divider shows as a colored strip with its name: between the facing pages, at the left edge of a left-hand page, or across the top of the page in Simplified editing.
- In Simplified editing, Alt+PgUp and Alt+PgDn go to the previous and next divider.

### Rename, move or remove a divider

In edit mode:

- Click the **×** on a tab to remove the divider. OmniCard asks first: click **Remove divider** to remove it, or **Cancel** to keep it. If you change your mind afterwards, click **Undo** in the message that appears.
- Right-click a tab for more:
  - **Rename or recolor…** changes its name or tab color.
  - **Move to page**, for example **Move to page 12**, moves it to where you are now (the same spot **Add divider** would use).
  - **Remove divider** removes it, after asking you to confirm.

### When pages change

Dividers stay with their page:

- Inserting a sheet moves the dividers after it along with their pages. A sheet inserted just before a divider joins the section in front of it.
- Removing the sheet a divider is on moves the divider to the next page, where its section now starts. If it was on the last sheet, it moves to the new last page.

## What the pockets show

Each pocket shows the card's artwork. Hover over a card to see its name, condition and whether it's foil. Foil cards have a moving rainbow sheen so you can spot them at a glance.

### Price and sale badges

Small badges on each card tell you about its value and sale status:

| Badge | Where | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Bottom left | The card's current market price. |
| **Listed** (blue) | Top left | The card is listed for sale. |
| **Picked** (orange) | Top left | The card is listed and has been picked into your for-sale location. |
| Channel and price | Bottom right | Where it's listed (Manual, TCGplayer or eBay) and the listed price. |

See [Sales](help:sales) for how listing and picking work.

### Card backs in empty pockets

A binder sheet has two sides, and each pocket on the front backs onto a pocket on the back. When a pocket is empty but the pocket behind it on the other side of the sheet holds a card, OmniCard shows that game's card back on a striped background. Hover over it to see *Card on the reverse side of this sheet*.

This matches what you'd see through a clear pocket, and helps you recognize the page you're holding.

## See a card's details

Click any card to open its details. From there you can edit its condition, foil, quantity, purchase price, note and tags, list it for sale, add it to a trade, split a stack, move it to another location, or delete it. See [Collection](help:collection).

You can also right-click a card and choose **Card details** or **List for sale**.

## Arrange cards in edit mode

Click **Edit** at the top right to start arranging the binder. The button changes to **Done editing**; click it when you're finished. Changes save as you make them.

If you don't see **Edit**, the binder is in a site you can only view. If changes show an error, you may not have permission to edit binders. Either way, ask an administrator for access.

![Edit mode with the Unplaced cards panel and edit toolbar](binders-edit-mode.webp)

In edit mode you get:

- An edit toolbar with **Add page**, **Layout** and **Remove page** buttons, the **Sets** filter, the **Foil if** box, and the **Simplified** switch. See [Search only some sets](#search-only-some-sets), [Make cards foil automatically](#make-cards-foil-automatically) and [Fill a binder from the keyboard](#fill-a-binder-from-the-keyboard).
- The **Unplaced cards** panel on the left, listing cards that are in this binder but not in a pocket yet.
- Dashed pocket outlines highlighting where you can drop cards.

### The Unplaced cards panel

Cards end up in the Unplaced pool when you move or import them into a binder without choosing a pocket, when you split a stack, or when you remove a page.

- The heading shows how many unplaced cards there are, for example *Unplaced cards (14)*.
- Each entry shows the card's set, number, condition, foil, market price and sale status.
- Use the search box to filter the list. It accepts the full search syntax, such as `set:mh3`, `t:dragon` or `tag:trade`. See [Search syntax](help:search-syntax).
- Click an entry to open its details, or right-click it for **Card details**, **List for sale** and **Delete from collection…**. Deleting asks first, then removes that copy from your collection for good. To keep a card but take it out of the binder, move it to another location instead.

When everything is placed, the panel says *Everything in this binder is placed.*

### Place, move and remove cards

- **Place a card**: drag it from the Unplaced cards panel onto a pocket.
- **Move a card**: drag it from one pocket to another, on the same spread.
- **Swap two cards**: drag a placed card onto an occupied pocket. The two cards trade places.
- **Replace a card from the pool**: drag an unplaced card onto an occupied pocket. The card that was there goes back to the Unplaced pool.
- **Remove a card from its pocket**: drag it from the pocket onto the Unplaced cards panel. The card stays in the binder; it just no longer has a pocket.

> [!TIP]
> To move a card to a pocket on a different spread, drag it to the Unplaced cards panel first, turn to the spread you want, then drag it into the pocket.

## Add or replace a card in a pocket

You can fill a specific pocket with a card from anywhere in your collection, or with a brand-new card from the catalog.

1. Right-click a pocket and choose **Add card to this pocket…** (or **Replace card in this pocket…** if it already holds a card).
2. The dialog shows the page and slot, and which card will be replaced.
3. Choose the **Game**, then type the card's name or its collector number in **Name or number**, and optionally fill in **Set** and **Collector #**. See [Search by collector number](#search-by-collector-number). For games whose catalog holds more than one language, each result shows its language, and **Languages** limits the results, as in [Add cards to a location](help:locations#add-cards-to-a-location).
4. Pick a result. Point at it to see a larger picture, or use ↓, ↑ and Enter from the search boxes:
   - **In your collection** lists copies you own in other locations, with the location each is in. Click one to move a single copy into the pocket right away. If it's part of a stack, only one copy moves.
   - **In the catalog** lists every matching printing. Click one, set **Condition**, **Language**, **Purchase price** and **Foil** (with its **Foil type**), then click **Add to pocket** to add a new card to your collection in this pocket.

![The Add card to pocket dialog](binders-add-to-pocket.png)

If the pocket already held a card, that card goes back to the Unplaced pool.

Once you've picked a printing, the keyboard moves to the picked card: press Enter to add it, or C, L, F and P to set **Condition**, **Language**, **Foil** and **Purchase price** first. See [Set card details from the keyboard](help:locations#set-card-details-from-the-keyboard). To choose a different printing, press Esc (or click **Change**) to go back to the results.

> [!NOTE]
> **In your collection** only lists copies in other locations. To place a copy that's already in this binder, drag it from the Unplaced cards panel.

### Search by collector number

Instead of a name, you can type the number printed on the card into **Name or number**. You don't need the **Collector #** box. The printings with that number are listed first, still limited by **Languages** and the sets you've chosen, so with the card's set chosen you usually get exactly the card in your hand.

- **Just digits**, such as `0019` or `19`: every printing whose own number is 19, however the game writes it, with or without leading zeros. That includes `19`, `019/165`, `OP01-019`, `2-019R` and `LOB-EN019`, but not `119` or `190`.
- **With letters**, such as `LOB-EN019`, `EN019`, `OP01-019`, `2-019R` or `OGN-019`: that number as printed. Capitals and leading zeros don't matter, and you can leave off a final rarity letter (`2-019` finds `2-019R`).

A card whose name looks like a number, such as *Porygon2*, is still found by its name.

> [!TIP]
> Choose the set first with the **Sets** filter (F2), then type the number from each card. See [Search only some sets](#search-only-some-sets).

## Fill a binder from the keyboard

**Simplified** editing is for filling a binder pocket by pocket without reaching for the mouse. It shows one page at a time, centered, and a cursor marks the selected pocket.

1. Click **Edit**, then turn on **Simplified** at the right of the edit toolbar. OmniCard remembers the switch in this browser, so next time **Edit** opens straight into it.
2. Move the cursor to a pocket with the arrow keys.
3. Start typing the card's name, or the collector number printed on it (see [Search by collector number](#search-by-collector-number)). The search dialog opens with what you typed. On an empty pocket you can also press Enter to open it empty.
4. Press Enter to pick the highlighted printing. To change its details, press C (condition), L (language), F (foil) or P (price), and Enter when you're done with each. Then press Enter again to add it.

The card is added to your collection in that pocket, the dialog closes, and the cursor moves on to the next pocket, ready for the next card.

![Simplified editing: one page with the cursor on a pocket, and the Unplaced cards panel beside it](binders-simplified.webp)

Above the page, next to the page number, OmniCard shows which side of which physical sheet you're on, for example *Page 2 of 4 · back of sheet 1*. A single-sided sheet shows as *sheet 3, one side*.

The buttons beside it turn the pages:

- **« First** goes to the front of page 1.
- **‹ Prev** and **Next ›** go back or on one page.
- **Last »** goes to the last page that has a card on it, whether that's the front or the back of its sheet, and puts the cursor on the pocket after the last card there, ready for the next one. Empty pages after it are skipped. If the last card is in the page's final pocket, the cursor goes on that card.

While the binder has cards that aren't in a pocket yet, the **Unplaced cards** panel is shown to the left of the page. It works as in normal edit mode: drag a card from it onto a pocket to place it, or drag a placed card onto the panel to take it out. When every card is placed, the panel goes away. The page stays in the same place whether the panel is shown or not. In a narrow window, the panel is below the page instead.

The line under the page names the selected pocket and what's in it, for example *Page 2 · pocket 1 — empty*. Below it is a reminder of the keys:

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| ← ↑ ↓ → | Move the cursor. → at the right edge of a page goes on to the next page, ← at the left edge goes back. |
| PgUp, PgDn | Go to the previous or next page. **‹ Prev** and **Next ›** above the page do the same. |
| Home, End | Go to the first or last pocket on the page. |
| Ctrl+Home, Ctrl+End | Go to the front of page 1, or to the last page with a card on it. **« First** and **Last »** do the same. |
| Alt+PgUp, Alt+PgDn | Go to the previous or next divider. See [Dividers](#dividers). |
| Enter | On a card: open its details, to correct its condition, language, foil and so on, or to delete it. On an empty pocket: search for a card. |
| A–Z, 0–9 | Start typing a name or collector number: the search opens with it. On a pocket that holds a card, the card you pick replaces it. |
| F2 | Choose the sets searches are limited to. See [Search only some sets](#search-only-some-sets). |
| Del | Take the card out of the pocket. It goes back to the Unplaced pool and stays in the binder. |
| Ctrl+C | Copy the card in this pocket. See [Copy a card into the next pockets](#copy-a-card-into-the-next-pockets). |
| Ctrl+V | Add a new copy of the copied card to this pocket, then move on to the next pocket. |
| + | Insert a new double-sided sheet (two pages) right after the sheet you're on, and move the cursor to its front. This only works from the back of a sheet (or a single-sided sheet), like slipping a new sheet into a real binder: on a front page, OmniCard tells you to turn to the back first. To add a sheet at the end, go to the last page and press +. |
| − | Remove the page the cursor is on. OmniCard asks first: press Enter to remove it or Esc to keep it. As with **Remove page**, the whole sheet goes, and its cards return to the Unplaced pool. |

You can use the mouse too: click a pocket to move the cursor to it. If the pocket holds a card, its details open too. Double-click an empty pocket to search. Right-click still opens the pocket menu, with **Replace card in this pocket…**.

### Copy a card into the next pockets

When several identical cards sit next to each other, enter the first one, then copy it into the rest:

1. Move the cursor to the card and press Ctrl+C. The line under the page shows what you copied, for example *Copied: The One Ring (LTR #697)*.
2. Move the cursor to the next pocket and press Ctrl+V. A new copy of the card is added to your collection in that pocket, with the same printing, foil and finish, condition, language and purchase price. The cursor moves on to the next pocket, so you can keep pressing Ctrl+V.

If the pocket already holds a card, the pasted card takes its place and the old one goes back to the Unplaced pool.

The card goes on your computer's clipboard, so Ctrl+V uses whatever you copied last. If you've copied some text since, OmniCard says *The clipboard doesn't hold a binder card.*: copy the card again. Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V in a text box, such as **Foil if** or the Unplaced cards search, copy and paste text as usual.

### The search dialog in Simplified editing

The dialog works as described in [Add or replace a card in a pocket](#add-or-replace-a-card-in-a-pocket), with a few changes to keep your hands on the keyboard:

- Only **In the catalog** results are shown. Copies you already own elsewhere aren't offered, because a binder you're filling this way is usually new cards.
- The first result is highlighted as soon as the results arrive, with its picture shown beside the dialog. Use ↑ and ↓ to move the highlight.
- If you press Enter before the results have arrived, OmniCard picks the top result when they do.
- Esc goes back from a picked printing to the results. With no printing picked, Esc closes the dialog.
- To set **Condition**, **Language**, **Foil** or **Purchase price** before adding, press C, L, F or P on the picked card. See [Set card details from the keyboard](help:locations#set-card-details-from-the-keyboard).

![The search dialog in Simplified editing, with the key reminder](binders-simplified-search.webp)

> [!TIP]
> To place a copy from the Unplaced pool, drag it from the **Unplaced cards** panel beside the page. To place one from another location, turn **Simplified** off and right-click the pocket.

## Search only some sets

When you're filling a binder from a few sets, you can limit card searches to those sets instead of typing a set code for every card. The filter works in normal and Simplified editing.

1. In edit mode, click **Sets: All sets** in the edit toolbar, or press F2.
2. Check the **Game**, then start typing a set's code or name in **Sets**: the matching sets are listed as you type. Press Enter to add the highlighted one. Add as many sets as you like.
3. Click **Done**, or press F2 again.

If the binder has default sets, the filter starts on them each time you open the binder, and **Binder's sets** puts them back after a change. To change the defaults, see [Game and default sets](help:locations#game-and-default-sets). If the binder is kept to one game, the filter's **Game** is locked to it (*This binder only holds this game*).

![Choosing the sets to search: two chosen, more matching what's typed](binders-set-filter.webp)

The button now lists the sets, for example **Sets: M21, DOM**. Every pocket search then starts limited to them: the dialog shows them as chips in **Sets**, and only cards from those sets are listed. The results come set by set, in the order you added the sets: with **M21, DOM**, every M21 card is listed before any DOM card. A change to the filter lasts until you leave the binder page. To go back to every set, open the filter and click **Clear**.

You can change the sets for one card without changing the filter:

- Remove or add chips in the dialog's **Sets** box. Press F2 to move between **Name or number** and **Sets**.
- Press Alt+A, or click **Search all sets**, to search every set. When nothing matches in the chosen sets, the dialog says so, for example *No matches in M21, DOM.*, and offers **Search all sets**.
- Type a set code in the **Set** box next to **Name or number**. It is used instead of the chosen sets, and finds only that set, for a collector number too. If what you type isn't a set code, sets whose name contains it are searched, for example *Eclipsed*.

The next pocket starts with the toolbar's sets again.

## Make cards foil automatically

If you keep foils of some cards in a binder, for example only the commons and uncommons, you can have OmniCard set **Foil** for you on every card you add to a pocket.

1. In edit mode, type a search in **Foil if** in the edit toolbar. It uses the same syntax as card searches, for example `r<rare` for Magic, or `r:common or r:uncommon` for any game. See [Search syntax](help:search-syntax).
2. Choose the **Finish** these cards get. The list shows the finishes of the game on screen. The first one is the game's usual foil.

![A picked common card, turned foil by the binder's foil rule](binders-foil-rule.webp)

When you pick a printing in the Add card dialog, OmniCard checks it against the search. If it matches, **Foil** is turned on with that finish, and the dialog says *Foil: matches the binder's foil rule*. If it doesn't match, **Foil** stays off. Either way you can still change **Foil** (or press F) before you add the card.

The check takes a moment. If you press Enter to add the card before it's done, the dialog says *Checking the foil rule…* and adds the card as soon as it knows.

OmniCard remembers the rule for this binder in this browser. To stop using it, clear the **Foil if** box.

> [!NOTE]
> The rule is checked against the exact printing you pick. Promotional printings can have a different rarity from the usual ones. For example, Magic's prerelease promos are listed as rare.

## Pages and layout

### Add pages

In edit mode, click **Add page** and choose:

- **Double-sided sheet**: adds a sheet with a front and a back (two pages).
- **Single-sided page**: adds a sheet with one usable side (one page).

**Double-sided sheet** and **Single-sided page** add the new sheet at the end of the binder.

### Insert a sheet between two others

When the spread you're looking at shows the back of one sheet on the left and the front of the next on the right, **Add page** also offers an option to insert a sheet there, for example **Double-sided sheet after page 2**. It slips a new, empty sheet in between, exactly where you'd add one in a real binder. The pages after it move up by two, and their cards move with them.

In Simplified editing, go to the back of the sheet you want the new one after, and press + (or use the same **Add page** option). See [Fill a binder from the keyboard](#fill-a-binder-from-the-keyboard).

The option isn't offered between the two sides of one sheet, because a new sheet can't go there.

### Change the layout

Use **Layout** to set how many pockets each page has. The choice applies to the whole binder.

| Layout | Pockets per page |
|---|---|
| 2 × 2 | 4 |
| 3 × 3 | 9 |
| 3 × 4 | 12 |
| 4 × 4 | 16 |

Most trading-card binder pages are 3 × 3.

### Remove a page

In edit mode, the toolbar shows a **Remove page** button for each page on the current spread (for example **Remove page 4** and **Remove page 5**).

Removing a page removes the whole physical sheet it's on. For a double-sided sheet, that's both sides. Cards on the removed sheet go back to the Unplaced pool, and the pages after it move up to close the gap. A binder must keep at least one page.

> [!WARNING]
> **Remove page** works immediately, without asking you to confirm. Check which sheet you're on before you click it.

## Troubleshooting

- **There's no Edit button.** The binder is in a site you can only view. Ask an administrator for write access.
- **Editing shows an error.** You may not have permission to edit binders. Ask an administrator.
- **A card I added isn't showing in a pocket.** It's in the Unplaced pool. Click **Edit** and look in the **Unplaced cards** panel.
- **The arrow keys don't move the cursor.** Simplified editing has to be on, and the keyboard mustn't be in a box or menu. Click the page, then try again.
- **Pressing + says the page is the front of its sheet.** A new sheet goes in after a sheet's back. Press PgDn to turn to the back, then press + again.
- **The search doesn't find a card I know exists.** The **Sets** filter may be leaving its set out. Press Alt+A in the dialog to search all sets, or clear the filter in the edit toolbar.
- **I can't see the Unplaced cards panel.** In Simplified editing it's only shown while some of the binder's cards aren't in a pocket. In a narrow window, scroll down: it's below the page.
- **Ctrl+V says the clipboard doesn't hold a binder card.** You copied something else after the card. Go back to the card, press Ctrl+C, and try again.
- **A card wasn't made foil.** Check the **Foil if** search against the printing you picked, for example its rarity. Promotional printings often differ.
- **Add divider put the divider in the wrong place.** In the two-page view it goes in front of the right-hand page. Turn to the right page, then right-click its tab and choose **Move to page**, or use Simplified editing to put it in front of one exact page.
- **A card I'm looking for isn't in the Unplaced panel.** Clear the panel's search box. If it still isn't there, it may be in another location; use **Add card to this pocket…** and look under **In your collection**.
