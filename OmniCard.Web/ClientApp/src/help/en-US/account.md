# Your account

The Account page is where you change your own email and password. Everyone can use it, whatever their permissions.

## Open your account

Click the **Account** button (the person icon) at the top right, then choose **My account**.

The **Profile** section shows your **Username** and the date you became a member. Only an administrator can change your username.

## Change your email

Your email is optional. If you have one, you can type it instead of your username when you sign in.

1. Under **Email**, type your new email address. To remove your email, clear the box.
2. Enter your **Current password**. OmniCard asks for it because your email can be used to sign in.
3. Click **Save email**. You'll see *Email saved.*, or *Email removed.* if you cleared it.

If you see *Current password is incorrect.*, check your password and try again. Each email can belong to only one account. If someone else already uses that email, OmniCard tells you.

## Change your password

1. Under **Password**, enter your **Current password**.
2. Enter a **New password**, then type it again in **Confirm new password**.
3. Click **Change password**. You'll see *Password changed.*

If the two new passwords don't match, the form says *Passwords don't match.*

> [!TIP]
> Forgotten your password? You can't change it here without the current one. Ask an administrator to require a password reset. They'll give you a setup key to enter when you next sign in. See [Sign-in problems](help:troubleshooting).
